Featured Novels

Sunken Sheriff

Underwater Fantasy · 2026

Beneath the waters of a long-drowned frontier town, Sheriff Baros patrols a ghostly community trapped between memory and oblivion. Bound by a mystical code where truth itself sustains his life, he keeps the peace among restless spirits who have forgotten they are dead. When a treasure hunter arrives seeking lost gold, old wounds and dangerous truths begin to surface. Sunken Sheriff is an atmospheric supernatural western that explores justice, greed and the fragile balance between the living and the dead.

Scrap-Iron Surgeon

Cyberpunk · 2026

In the scrapyards beneath a towering corporate dystopia, a young mechanic named Flux discovers the damaged AI core of a discarded combat machine. Determined to give it a second chance, he builds a powerful new body from forgotten scraps and uncovers a sinister corporate secret. As a brutal mech tournament looms, Flux and his unlikely allies challenge a system that treats sentient machines as disposable tools. Scrap-Iron Surgeon is a cyberpunk adventure about resilience, empathy and the fight for freedom in a world ruled by profit.
